About Me

A quick introduction to my academic journey and interests

I am currently a 3rd-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student. This portfolio serves as a central hub documenting my laboratory exercises, assignments, and practical coding projects for our course: IT31 - Web Systems and Technologies.

My focus is on strengthening my mastery over core web standards (HTML5 semantics, modern CSS layouts, and vanilla JavaScript), while developing clean coding habits, modular project structure, and responsive design principles.

  • Focus Areas: Frontend Web Development, UI/UX Structure
